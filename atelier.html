Atelier Helix

Your keys. Your quota. Your canvas.

Atelier is an original Helix design studio inspired by Lovart’s public product (chat + infinite board + thinking/fast agent). Model calls go to your official OpenAI and Gemini accounts — not Lovart credits.

Launch locally:

python3 -m atelier
# open http://127.0.0.1:8765

Set OPENAI_API_KEY and/or GEMINI_API_KEY, or paste keys in the studio rail. Demo mode weaves SVG with zero spend.
